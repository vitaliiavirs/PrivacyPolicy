Privacy Policy

Cinelog

This policy explains, point by point, how Cinelog treats your information.

01

Cinelog does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There is no sign-up and no account, so you are never asked who you are.

02

The content you create — titles, ratings, notes, genres, episode progress and favorites — is stored only in the app's local database on your device.

03

Cinelog makes no network connections. It has no servers of its own and contacts no third parties. Nothing you enter leaves your iPhone.

04

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking or SDKs.

05

Cinelog requests no device permissions — not your camera, photos, location, contacts, or notifications.

06

You are in full control of deletion. "Erase all titles" in Settings removes everything instantly, and deleting the app removes its database entirely. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete or return on our end.

Contact for privacy questions: vitalii@avirservice.pro

Last updated: 09.11.2026
